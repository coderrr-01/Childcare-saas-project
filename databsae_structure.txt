Key Design Decisions
Decision	Rationale
BIGINT over UUID	Better index performance, smaller storage, natural ordering
CHECK constraints over ENUMs	Flexibility for future status additions
Separate daily care tables	Proper querying per care type, not a generic daily_care
Polymorphic media_links	Links media to children, stories, incidents without separate tables
incident_status_history	Full workflow audit trail, not just a status column
medication_administrations	Separate log of each dose administered
No schema separation	Public schema with tenant IDs is simpler for Phase 1
